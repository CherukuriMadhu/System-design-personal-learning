**GoF** stands for **Gang of Four**.

In software engineering, the **Gang of Four** refers to the four authors of the famous book:

> **“Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software”**

### The 4 authors

1. **Erich Gamma**
2. **Richard Helm**
3. **Ralph Johnson**
4. **John Vlissides**

They are collectively called the **Gang of Four (GoF)**.

### Why is GoF important in LLD?

Their book introduced the **23 classic design patterns**, divided into 3 categories:

| Category       | Patterns | Purpose                       |
| -------------- | -------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Creational** | 5        | Object creation               |
| **Structural** | 7        | Object/class relationships    |
| **Behavioral** | 11       | Object communication/behavior |

So when someone says:

> **"GoF Design Patterns"**

they usually mean these **23 classic patterns**.

For interviews, you can say:

> **“GoF refers to the Gang of Four—the four authors who documented 23 classic object-oriented design patterns. These patterns are categorized into creational, structural, and behavioral patterns and are widely used in LLD.”**
